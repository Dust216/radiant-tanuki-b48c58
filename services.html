CHOOSING A SERVICE

Start with the reason you need an inspection

Some inspections evaluate general property condition; others address a particular insurance, financing, or environmental question. Confirm the requested report type with your insurer, lender, or other requesting party before booking.

01

02

A 4-point or wind mitigation report serves a different purpose than a full home inspection.

03

Specialized testing

Sampling, laboratory analysis, and WDO documentation can have separate scopes or provider requirements.
