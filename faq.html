HELP CENTER

Common inspection questions

What types of inspections do you offer?

We offer residential, wind mitigation and insurance, mold and indoor air quality, WDO, environmental testing, and commercial inspection options. See our Services page for details.

What areas do you serve?

Brevard, St. Lucie, Martin, Palm Beach, and Broward Counties in South Florida.

How do I schedule an inspection?

Use the Schedule Now link or call or text (772) 272-1600 to discuss your needs.

How much does an inspection cost?

Pricing depends on the property and inspection scope. Request a quote with your property details for an accurate price.

Will I receive an inspection report?

Inspection services include documentation appropriate to the agreed scope. Ask about report format and delivery timing when booking.

Do you offer wind mitigation and 4-point inspections?

Yes, those are among the insurance-related services listed. Confirm your insurer’s required forms and property details when booking.

Do I need to attend the inspection?

Ask when scheduling. Attendance arrangements can depend on the inspection type and property access.

What should I prepare before the appointment?

Have the property address, service requested, access instructions, and any deadline or lender/insurer requirements available.

Is a wind mitigation inspection the same as a 4-point inspection?

No. They serve different insurance documentation purposes. Ask your insurer which report or forms they require before booking.

Can I request more than one inspection service?

Yes, you can describe all the services you need in your inspection request. Confirm availability, scope, and pricing before the appointment.

Do you guarantee that an inspection will lower my insurance premium?

No. Insurance pricing and eligibility are determined by your insurance company. An inspection provides documentation that an insurer may consider.

Can you inspect a property before I make an offer?

Contact us to discuss your timeline and property access. Inspection scheduling depends on availability and authorization to enter the property.

What if a report identifies a concern?

Review the observation and consider follow-up with an appropriately qualified contractor or specialist. An inspection does not replace a repair estimate or specialist evaluation.

HELPFUL TO KNOW

A few important distinctions

The right inspection depends on why it is being requested. A general home inspection, insurance form, and specialized laboratory test are not interchangeable.

01

Home inspections

Focus on accessible property systems and components under an agreed scope.

02

Insurance inspections

May require specific forms requested by the insurer.

03

Specialized testing

May involve separate sampling, laboratories, and reporting procedures.
